# MB25 research comparison v1.0 — not the production MB/E&V engine
Frozen rule design before receiving the 25-stock collection. This is an isolated research score, not a replacement methodology or a reproduction of archived scores. No optimization against subsequent returns or legacy headline scores.

Common equity-price cutoff: 2026-09-04 regular-session close. Public information retrieved through 2026-09-06. Same thresholds for incumbents and candidates. No per-group percentiles or min-max rescaling. Arithmetic is deterministic conditional on the documented analyst assessments.

## MB research factor weights
TAM 15%; growth trajectory 15%; operating/free-cash-flow economics 10%; balance sheet 10%; dilution 10%; moat 15%; execution 10%; valuation 15%. These are the existing documented eight weights, but the input mappings below are NEW RESEARCH mappings and must not be labeled official scores.

Quantitative scores use piecewise linear interpolation clipped to [0,100].
- Growth: 50% latest issuer organic revenue growth when explicitly available (otherwise vendor latest consolidated growth if organic comparability is not impaired), 50% next-fiscal-year consensus revenue growth. Document material acquisition distortions and exclude the distorted current-growth input rather than invent organic growth. Growth anchors: -20%:0, 0%:25, 10%:50, 20%:70, 40%:90, 60%:100. Revenue-base below $25m caps growth at 80. Missing inputs are excluded with disclosed coverage, not replaced with zero.
- Economics: equal weight TTM GAAP operating margin and conventional TTM CFO-minus-capex / revenue. Operating-margin anchors -50%:0, -20%:20, 0%:50, 10%:75, 20%:90, 30%:100. FCF-margin anchors -100%:0, -30%:15, -10%:35, 0%:50, 10%:75, 20%:90, 30%:100. Use conventional capex, not an issuer metric excluding growth spending. Prefer four consecutive quarterly statements; annotate fallback provider TTM metrics. Do not sum overlapping annual/YTD periods.
- Balance: positive TTM EBITDA uses (total debt - cash)/EBITDA. Anchors -1:100, 0:90, 1:80, 2:65, 3:50, 4:35, 6:10, 8:0. Negative EBITDA uses cash runway in months at trailing conventional FCF burn, anchors 0:0, 6:20, 12:40, 24:70, 36:85, 60:100. If neither metric reliable, score remains missing. Restricted cash must not be included knowingly.
- Dilution: change in comparable diluted weighted-average shares, latest quarter versus year-ago quarter; use issuer/verified vendor year-on-year count where quarterly series unavailable. Split adjustments require explicit check. Anchors -5%:100, 0%:90, 5%:70, 10%:50, 20%:25, 40%:5, 60%:0.
- Valuation: 60% enterprise-value / forward gross profit, plus 40% conventional TTM FCF / equity capitalization. Forward gross profit = next-fiscal-year consensus revenue * trailing gross margin; this is a CONSTANT-MARGIN SCENARIO, not a forecast of actual gross profit. Forward-GP multiple anchors 1:100, 3:90, 5:80, 10:60, 20:35, 40:10, 60:0. FCF-yield anchors -10%:0, -2%:20, 0%:40, 2%:60, 5%:80, 8%:95, 10%:100. Negative/zero actual gross profit gets zero for that valuation component (not missing data). Nonpositive EV is separately flagged; no artificial infinite bargain. Financial-company gross-margin/EV and working-capital cash flow limitations must be visible.

TAM, moat, execution are SOURCE-INFORMED ANALYST ASSESSMENTS, not automatically extracted facts. Grades are 0,25,50,75,100 with half-steps (12.5 points) allowed. TAM: 0 structural contraction,25 narrow challenged demand,50 durable modest expansion,75 credible broad secular growth,100 exceptional broad high-growth runway. Moat:0 none/broken,25 early unproved differentiation,50 defendable niche with substantial competition,75 demonstrated switching/qualification/IP/cost barriers,100 established exceptional structural advantage. Execution:0 broken credibility,25 early unproven commercialization,50 mixed delivery/integration,75 repeated delivery with caveats,100 long consistent growth/margins/cash generation. Every grade needs a short evidence-linked rationale. The model intentionally rewards demonstrated business economics; it is not an estimated probability of a fivefold return.

## Research E&V
70% valuation factor +30% forward EPS revision score. Revision = (current FY+1 consensus EPS / consensus EPS 90 days ago)-1, only for a strictly positive baseline. Anchors -20%:0,-10%:20,0%:50,+10%:80,+20%:100. Missing or negative-baseline EPS revisions remain unscored; disclose component coverage and do not imply equivalence to official E&V.

## Technical research overlay
Independent 0-100 score, never part of MB ranking. 30% trend,20% momentum,20% relative-strength/volume,20% support/risk,10% price confirmation. This uses new explicit research mappings, not a claimed reproduction of dashboard v2.2. Trend:30/30/40 points for close above MA20/50/200. Momentum:40% RSI mapped 30:0,50:50,65:100,80:50,90:0;40% MACD histogram scaled by ATR via clip(50+250*hist/ATR);20% signed directional ADX via clip(50+50*sign(+DI-minusDI)*min(ADX,50)/50). RS/volume:75% average 1M/3M total-return excess over equal-weight QQQ and SMH mapped clip(50+200*excess);25% volume confirmation score clip(50+25*(volume20ratio-1)*sign(close/MA20-1)). Support/risk:50% clip(100-25*abs(close-MA20)/ATR),50% close position in 20-day range. Confirmation:50% close position in 20-day range,50% trend-confirmed volume above. Zero denominators/missing required fields remain missing.

## Ranking, sensitivity and missingness
Primary rank: unrounded research MB descending, ties alphabetical. Report one decimal; differences under 3 points not decisive. Unknown factors are excluded from numeric denominator, but scores with less than 90% factor-weight coverage are NOT promotion-eligible. Show lower/upper bounds treating unknown factors as 0/100.
Two prespecified alternate weight vectors for sensitivity: growth emphasis TAM20 growth25 economics10 balance5 dilution5 moat15 execution5 valuation15; quality emphasis TAM10 growth10 economics15 balance15 dilution15 moat15 execution10 valuation10. Also stress the three analyst factors by +/-12.5 points, clipped at 0/100; these are judgment stress ranges, not confidence intervals. No list replacements in this study.
