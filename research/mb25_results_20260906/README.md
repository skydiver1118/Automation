## Rules

Weights: TAM15%, growth15%, economics10%, balance10%, dilution10%, moat15%, execution10%, valuation15%. TAM/moat/execution are explicit, source-informed analyst grades (40% of headline weight). All quantitative scores use the same clipped piecewise-linear anchors across 25 stocks, not per-group percentiles.

Growth combines comparable current growth with FY+1 consensus growth. Economics combines reported operating margin and conventional CFO-minus-total-capex margin. Balance uses an operating-EBITDA leverage proxy or cash runway. Dilution uses comparable YoY diluted share counts. Valuation combines 60% EV/forward-gross-profit scenario and 40% conventional FCF yield. Forward gross profit holds trailing gross margin constant on next-fiscal-year consensus revenue; it is a scenario, not an actual forecast. E&V combines 70% valuation and 30% positive-baseline EPS revisions. Missing components are excluded with disclosed coverage and uncertainty bounds. Technical 30/20/20/20/10 is separate from headline MB.
